Before you ask

Common questions

Is payment required?
No — our engagements are free. We do this for the experience: real problems make our members better consultants, and your results are the payoff.
What's the timeline?
We're flexible — we shape the engagement around whatever fits you and your company. A typical semester project runs about 10 weeks with regular check-ins, a midpoint presentation, and a final presentation.
Who will be working on my project?
A hand-picked team of a project leader, a senior consultant, and four consultants — backed by advisors in professional service industries. All are UC Berkeley students.
What do we get at the end?
Deliverables tailored to your problem — slide decks, written reports, quantitative models, or white papers — plus an implementation roadmap so you can move forward.
What kinds of problems do you take on?
Market expansion, growth strategy, data & analytics, and operations — for startups, nonprofits, and established firms alike. If you're not sure your problem fits, ask.
How do we get started?
Send us a message above or email Berkeleyccg@gmail.com. We'll set up a short intro call to scope the engagement together.
