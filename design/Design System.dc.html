05 · Rules
· Violet is an accent, never a background for text-heavy sections. Dark sections use Ink or Deep.
· One gradient in the whole brand: the logo. Never gradient backgrounds.
· Corners: 8px buttons, 10px chips, 14px cards. Borders are 1px #E6E1EE hairlines.
· Photos of the club are the imagery. No illustrations, no stock clip-art.
· Section rhythm: eyebrow → headline → body → action. 120px vertical spacing between sections.
